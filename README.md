# Lethal Company Takeover

**Quake** × Lethal Company · visual · idea by the team

Bring Lethal Company into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I put Lethal Company in Quake: dark fog, Brackens, thieving Hoarding Bugs and a scrap quota. The Company horn is very loud.

## Install

Copy this mod folder into your Quake folder next to id1 (LibreQuake or Quake data needed), then launch Quake with -game followed by the folder name. Kill monsters, grab the scrap, meet the quota.
